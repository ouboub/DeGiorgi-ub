7 Moser Iteration and Supersolution Estimates

7.1 Moser Iteration

Throughout this section we fix a dimension \(d \in \mathbb {N}\) with \(d \geq 1\) and \(d {\gt} 2\), and write \(E = \mathbb {R}^d\) for the ambient space. All balls are taken around the origin in \(E\), and we abbreviate \(B_{r} = B_{r}(0)\). Functions \(u : E \to \mathbb {R}\) are real valued; \(A\) denotes a normalized elliptic coefficient field on \(B_{1}\), with ellipticity ratio \(\Lambda = \Lambda _A \geq 1\) NormalizedEllipticCoeff. The notion of subsolution is IsSubsolution and that of supersolution is IsSupersolution. The starting point is the normalized De Giorgi \(L^2 \to L^\infty \) estimate proved in Section 5.

Let \(d {\gt} 2\), \(A\) a normalized elliptic coefficient on \(B_{1}\), \(u\) a subsolution of \(A\), and \(\eta \in C^{\infty }_c(B_{s})\) a smooth cutoff with \(0 \leq \eta \leq 1\) and \(\| \nabla \eta \| _{L^\infty } \leq C_\eta \). Suppose \(0 {\lt} s \leq 1\), \(1 {\lt} p\), and \(\int _{B_{s}} |u_+|^p {\lt} \infty \). Then there exists a \(W^{1,2}\) witness for \(v := \mathrm{PC}_d(\eta ,u,p)\) on \(B_{s}\) satisfying

\begin{equation*} \int _{B_{s}} \| \nabla v\| ^{2}\, dx \; \leq \; 2\, C_\eta ^{2}\, \Big(\Lambda \, \Big(\frac{p}{p-1}\Big)^{2} + 1\Big) \int _{B_{s}} |u_+|^{p}\, dx. \end{equation*}
Proof ▼

The witness for \(v := \mathrm{PC}_d(\eta , u, p) = \eta \, (u_+)^{p/2}\) is constructed in moserPowerCutoff_memW1pWitness, which we now unpack. First, a \(W^{1,2}\) witness for \((u_+)^{p/2}\) on \(B_{s}\) is obtained from the truncation \(u_+\) (whose witness comes from positivePartSub_memW1pWitness_on_ball) composed with the power \(t \mapsto t^{p/2}\) via the \(W^{1,2}\) chain rule sobolev_chain_rule_unitBall; the local integrability \(\int (u_+)^{p} {\lt} \infty \) together with \(p {\gt} 1\) ensures the chain-rule hypotheses (locally Lipschitz factor on \([0,\infty )\)). The product \(\eta \, (u_+)^{p/2}\) is then an honest \(W^{1,2}\) function via MemW1pWitness.mul_smooth_bounded_p, with weak gradient \(\nabla v = \eta \, (p/2)(u_+)^{p/2-1}\nabla u_+ + (u_+)^{p/2}\nabla \eta \).

For the energy bound, test the subsolution inequality against \(\varphi := \eta ^{2}(u_+)^{p-1}\) (this is in \(H^{1}_{0}(B_{s})\) thanks to the support inclusion of \(\eta \) and the integrability hypothesis on \(u_+\)). The pointwise expansion of \(\langle A\nabla u, \nabla \varphi \rangle \) via bilinFormIntegrand_mul_smooth_eq yields

\begin{multline*} \int _{B_{s}} (p-1)\, \eta ^{2}\, (u_+)^{p-2}\, \langle A\nabla u_+, \nabla u_+\rangle \, dx \\ \le -2\int _{B_{s}} \eta \, (u_+)^{p-1}\, \langle A\nabla u_+, \nabla \eta \rangle \, dx, \end{multline*}

where on the left we discarded the supersolution-favourable term and on the right we used the subsolution inequality. The right-hand side is bounded by Cauchy–Schwarz against \(\langle A\cdot , \cdot \rangle \) followed by AM–GM with weight \(\varepsilon = (p-1)/2\):

\begin{equation*} 2|\eta \, (u_+)^{p-1}\langle A\nabla u_+,\nabla \eta \rangle | \le \frac{p-1}{2}\eta ^{2}(u_+)^{p-2}\langle A\nabla u_+,\nabla u_+\rangle + \frac{2}{p-1}(u_+)^{p}\langle A\nabla \eta ,\nabla \eta \rangle . \end{equation*}

Absorbing the first term into the left-hand side leaves \((p-1)/2 \cdot \int \eta ^{2}(u_+)^{p-2}\langle A\nabla u_+,\nabla u_+\rangle \), and the residual term is bounded by \(\Lambda C_\eta ^{2}\int (u_+)^{p}\) via the upper ellipticity bound for \(A\) and \(\| \nabla \eta \| \le C_\eta \).

Finally, expanding \(\nabla v = \eta \, (p/2)(u_+)^{p/2-1}\nabla u_+ + (u_+)^{p/2}\nabla \eta \), the pointwise inequality \(\| \nabla v\| ^{2} \le 2\, \eta ^{2}(p/2)^{2}(u_+)^{p-2}\| \nabla u_+\| ^{2} + 2(u_+)^{p}\| \nabla \eta \| ^{2}\) together with the absorbed Caccioppoli estimate gives

\begin{equation*} \int _{B_{s}} \| \nabla v\| ^{2}\, dx \le 2\, C_\eta ^{2}\Bigl(\Lambda \bigl(\tfrac {p}{p-1}\bigr)^{2} + 1\Bigr) \int _{B_{s}} (u_+)^{p}\, dx, \end{equation*}

the displayed energy inequality.

7.2.4 Stage two (forward low-power iteration)

The forward iteration is run only finitely many steps: starting from \(p_0 := q\, \chi ^{-m}\) for a target exponent \(q \in (0,1)\) and a depth \(m\) chosen so that \(p_0 \leq p\), we iterate \(m+1\) times to reach the level \(p_m = q\). The forward step constant

\begin{equation*} \mathrm{S}_n^{\mathrm{fwd}}(A,p_0) := \Big(\frac{C_{\mathrm{MA}}(d)}{(r_n-r_{n+1})^{2}}\, \Big(\Lambda \Big(\frac{p_n}{1-p_n}\Big)^{2}+1\Big)\Big)^{1/p_n} \end{equation*}

superStepConstFwd is finite as long as \(p_n {\lt} 1\), which is guaranteed by \(p_n \leq q {\lt} 1\) for \(n \leq m\).
