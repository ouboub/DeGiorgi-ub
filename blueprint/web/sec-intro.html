1 Introduction

This document is a complete natural-language translation of the Lean 4 formalization of the De Giorgi–Nash–Moser regularity theory for uniformly elliptic divergence-form equations with bounded measurable coefficients. The formalization is completely sorry-free and built on top of Mathlib. The Lean source is available at https://github.com/scottnarmstrong/DeGiorgi.

The main results formalized are:

  1. Local boundedness of weak subsolutions via De Giorgi iteration;

  2. Weak Harnack inequality for nonnegative weak supersolutions via Moser iteration;

  3. Harnack inequality for weak solutions;

  4. Interior Hölder regularity for weak solutions via Moser’s method.

Throughout, we work in dimension \(d \ge 3\) with functions \(u \colon \mathbb {R}^d \to \mathbb {R}\). We write \(B_{r} = \{ x \in \mathbb {R}^d : |x| {\lt} r\} \) for the open ball of radius \(r\) centered at the origin, and \(B_{r}(x_0)\) for the ball centered at \(x_0\).

Each definition and theorem includes a reference to its Lean name in typewriter font, enabling cross-referencing with the source code.

Notation. We adopt the following conventions:

  • \(\nabla u\) denotes the weak gradient of \(u\);

  • \((u - k)_+ = \max (u - k, 0)\) denotes the positive part;

  • \(\| f\| _{L^{p}(\Omega )}\) denotes the \(L^{p}\) norm over \(\Omega \);

  • \(\lambda , \Lambda {\gt} 0\) denote the ellipticity constants (lower and upper bounds);

  • \(|E|\) denotes the Lebesgue measure of a measurable set \(E\);

  • \(\mathop{⨍}\nolimits _\Omega f\, dx = |\Omega |^{-1}\int _\Omega f\, dx\) denotes the average integral.
